About Arquipoza

Arquipoza was founded in 2010 with a clear vision: to create architecture that goes beyond aesthetics, creating spaces that truly transform the lives of those who inhabit them.

Based in Madrid, our studio brings together a multidisciplinary team of architects, designers and urban planners united by a shared passion for innovation, sustainability and excellence.
